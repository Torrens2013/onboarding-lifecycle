  • Root Cause Analysis
  • Workforce Analysis
  • SWOT Analysis
  • Internal Benchmarking

Develop redesigned onboarding materials and activities based on the analysis above.

Ensure alignment with business goals and strategic objectives, addressing any identified gaps or weaknesses.

Incorporate best practices from the internal benchmarking analysis.

📅 Phase 3: Testing & Refinement – July 1st – July 15th, 2025

Pilot the redesigned onboarding process and collect feedback.

Revisit the root causes and SWOT analysis to make necessary adjustments.

Officially implement the new onboarding process across all teams.

Conduct a final review to ensure all business analysis tools have been considered and incorporated into the final process.
